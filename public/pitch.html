Technology can help surface a concern.

02Narrow by design

03Expand on evidence

A RESPONSIBLE WAY TO BEGIN

One proof point.
Then decide what’s next.

The long-term vision can be province-wide. The first technical step should be small enough to measure and safe enough to learn from.

PHASE 01
⌖

Agree on the use case

Choose one observable event with child-protection professionals. Define where cameras are never used and who may review any signal.

PHASE 02
◎

Test in a controlled setting

Work with an approved Lahore pilot site and an authorized technical team. Use only footage and data approved for that purpose.

PHASE 03
↗

Measure before expanding

Check missed events, false alarms, review time, staff experience, privacy, and security. Expand only if the evidence supports it.

i

What the prototype does not claim: it has no trained model, camera integration, official data, or validated performance. The pilot would establish whether the proposed capability is feasible and useful.

BUILT AROUND CHILD SAFETY

Safeguards are part of the proposal—not a later add-on.

Any real system would need the government’s child-protection, privacy, and technical teams to set the rules before a pilot begins.

✓

No automated abuse verdictsAI may flag an observable event; authorized people make decisions.

✓

No face-based identity or emotion scoringDo not infer who a child is or how they feel from their face.

✓

Limited access and retentionSet strict permissions, purpose limits, and deletion rules before collecting data.

✓

Clear human responseEvery alert needs a responsible reviewer and an agreed response process.
